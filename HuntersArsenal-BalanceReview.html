Formula: effective chance = p + (1 − p) × c. The normal drop rolls first; the bonus rolls only after failure. Only the highest eligible bonus applies. An Arsenal weapon's 25% therefore overrides a Bulwark's 20%, while Guard's 40% overrides both.

Multiple physical damage channels

  • Fang/Jaw divide their physical share between slash and pierce.
  • Bite/Fury retain separate Q1–Q7 chop values: 45 / 55 / 60 / 65 / 75 / 85 / 95.
  • Ruin retains separate chop values: 44 / 44 / 54 / 64 / 74 / 74 / 74.
  • Chop is excluded from creature-damage budgets because it targets trees and eligible structures.

Element behavior

  • Frost slows; Wet increases frost damage by 1.5×.
  • Poison does not stack; only a stronger remaining application replaces it.
  • Spirit stacks, but most creatures and all players are immune.
  • Lightning is direct damage; Wet increases it by 1.5×. No chain proc is included.
  • Fire stacks into a five-second burn; Wet and many Ashlands enemies resist it.
