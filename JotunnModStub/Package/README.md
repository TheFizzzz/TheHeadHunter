# Hunter's Arsenal

A fourteen-item weapon and shield family that grows from the Black Forest through the Deep North while improving trophy hunting.

## Features

- Seven qualities gated by the previous Forsaken trophy and current biome material.
- Ten unique elemental profiles targeting regional quality-2.5 vanilla damage.
- A configurable second trophy roll on Arsenal killing blows.
- Hunter's Guard and Hunter's Bulwark hunting marks.
- Matching-quality dual axe and dual dagger crafting.

## Installation

Requires BepInEx and Jötunn. Place `HuntersArsenal.dll` in `BepInEx/plugins/HuntersArsenal`.

## Known issues

Bow attribution checks the equipped weapon when prey dies, so switching during arrow flight can misattribute the trophy bonus.
